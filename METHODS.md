# Analysis specification

## Company-level comparison (Table 7)

The additional descriptive table uses the unchanged 1,002-filing regression
sample. `src/company_comparison.py` recounts both dictionaries in the retained
texts and verifies their shares against the saved tone measures. Repeated word
occurrences count separately; this is not a count of distinct dictionary words.

For issuer i and form f, the primary intensity measure is the arithmetic mean
of each filing's category count divided by its total tokens, multiplied by 100.
Annual and quarterly reports have separate rankings. Cumulative volume is the
sum of category occurrences across both forms; it depends on document length
and the number of retained filings. `company_summary.csv` also supplies the
pooled token-weighted share, total tokens, first and last filing dates, filing
counts and distinct calendar-year counts for each issuer-form-category group.

There is no additional minimum-observation filter or imputation. Observed top
five ranks include ties, with CIK ordering for display only. Sparse coverage
remains visible: CrowdStrike's retained observations are three 2025 quarters,
and Oklo's annual mean is one filing. Comparisons within forms still differ in
year coverage. These are descriptive sample rankings, without rank-difference
tests, industry controls or an inference that dictionary intensity measures
economic risk. SEC names and ticker labels are those in the retrieved metadata,
not a reconstruction of historical company names. The original regression
sample, IDF and inferential specifications are unchanged by this extension.

The implementation is in `src/tone.py`, `src/events.py`, `src/analysis.py` and
`src/corporate_actions.py`. Empirical outputs are generated by the notebook.

## Implemented: tone

Keep the supplied tokenizer: uppercase alphabetic tokens of length at least two,
allowing internal apostrophes and hyphens. Keep its numeric-table cutoff of 15%
of non-space characters. This is the starter parser's definition, not an exact
replication of the original paper's parsing pipeline.

For category C, proportional tone is sum(count[w], w in C) / total tokens.
It measures negativity or uncertainty intensity; a higher negative score means
more negative language. Do not reverse its sign and call it optimism.

For a word occurring in filing j, use

`w_ij = (1 + ln(count_ij)) / (1 + ln(a_j)) * ln(N / df_i)`.

Here `a_j = total tokens / distinct observed tokens`, interpreting the paper's
"average word count" as average term frequency within the document. Sum these
weights within each category. Do not add sklearn's smoothing or L2 normalization,
and do not divide the weighted sum by document length a second time.

Fit N and document frequency on the same final corpus used for the regressions.
This full-sample IDF is a descriptive research construction, not a point-in-time
trading signal. Any separate robustness corpus must have its IDF recomputed.

The downloaded dictionary has 2,355 Negative and 297 Uncertainty words, with
40 in both. Thus the categories are conceptually different but not disjoint.

## Implemented design decisions

- Use an exchange trading calendar with actual session closes, including early
  closes. Day 0 is the first session whose close follows acceptance; at or after
  the close, move to the next session. Report the number moved from filing date.
- Four-day excess return: stock buy-and-hold return over [0,3] minus SPY's
  buy-and-hold return over the same sessions, using adjusted prices.
- Realised-volatility windows: [-60,-6] and [1,63] trading days,
  annualised daily sample standard deviations. The post period approximates the
  following quarter; do not describe this as the paper's original yearly window.
- Size uses actual historical unadjusted price times contemporaneous shares;
  adjusted total-return prices are for returns, not historical market cap.
  Weighted-average diluted shares are not identical to cover-page shares and
  are excluded. Yahoo Close is split-adjusted even when auto_adjust=False.
  Observed split ratios through retrieval day restore as-traded prices and
  volume; shares and pre-window volumes are then expressed in the day -1 share
  units. See https://github.com/ranaroussi/yfinance/discussions/1682 .
- Use firm fixed effects and form controls for within-firm comparisons. Aggregate
  quarterly regressions need Newey-West inference because observations are serially
  dependent. Use four HAC lags with a finite-sample correction and t inference.
  Within-firm trends include firm-form effects and quarter-of-year seasonality.
  Panel covariance clusters on CIK and filing quarter; t inference uses the
  smaller cluster count minus one. No covariance eigenvalue/diagonal correction
  is applied. Undefined standard errors are retained as undefined.

Sequential sample filters deduplicate accession, restrict forms and dates,
require at least 2,000 tokens, valid acceptance/day 0, unambiguous exact-accession
cover-page shares, a previous-session as-traded price of at least USD 3,
complete event/pre/post return windows and complete positive volume, and finite
size/turnover. Missing previous-session prices are excluded before the USD 3
threshold. GOOG and GOOGL share a CIK: 92 eligible listings correspond to 91
companies, and their duplicate filings must not count twice. The first ticker in
the supplied sorted enumeration (GOOG) is retained by accession deduplication.

The primary pooled regressions share one final corpus and one IDF definition.
Form-specific tables are subgroup comparisons on those same measures and scales,
not independently redefined dictionaries. Quarterly analysis gives one vote to
each firm-form-quarter by averaging multiple eligible filings in a cell.

Volatility and return controls are log historical size, log mean daily pre-window
turnover, log tokens, pre-window stock-minus-SPY buy-and-hold return, filing-quarter
fixed effects, and form effects for pooled models. Volatility compares versions
with and without log pre-volatility on identical rows; the return regression
always includes it. Standardise tone on the common final sample, including when
presenting subgroups. No winsorisation, imputation, automatic outlier deletion,
or significance-based specification selection is used.

## Security identities and exclusions

The frozen input contains 226 fund positions and 130 distinct security records.
`src/issuer_map.py` maps the complete original ticker, CUSIP/SEDOL field and
company name to a reviewed SEC CIK. A changed or unknown identity stops the
universe build and requires review. This preserves Discovery's DSY and
Dassault Systemes' DSY FP as separate securities and maps AIR to Airbus rather
than AAR. Bare tickers cannot identify companies across exchanges.

SEC submissions checked on 12 September 2026 UTC establish 123 matched security
records. Of these, 31 have no 10-K or 10-Q in the 2021-2025 window; 92 listings
remain, representing 91 CIKs. The other seven records comprise four funds and
three foreign holdings without a verified SEC issuer match: Discovery Ltd,
LY Corporation and JD Logistics. Their issuer/exchange evidence appears in the
mapping and the Table 1 appendix. A missing match does not prove that an issuer
has no SEC records. I do not substitute a parent company or unrelated US ticker.

The starter's 124-code count combined five dropped securities (three numeric
codes and two ETFs) with one collision between the two DSY names. I reconcile
the full 130-record input instead. New analysis starts with 1,683 eligible
filing-listing records, removes 20 duplicate accessions, then applies the stated
sample filters. The final corpus has 1,002 filings from 60 firms.

The default universe script re-enumerates filings from SEC for the reviewed CIKs.
`--from-local-filings` checks the cached form counts against verified enumeration
counts before reusing the downloaded corpus. Original HTML and prices remain
cached; the analysis rejects metadata outside the reviewed issuer universe.

The return-power display uses `(t_0.975, min(Gfirm,Gquarter)-1 + z_0.8) * SE` as
an approximate 80%-power minimum detectable coefficient at two-sided 5% size.
It is a design-sensitivity calculation, not observed power computed from an
estimated effect. A lack of return precision is not a blanket claim that trends
and volatility regressions are underpowered.

In the 10-K within-firm trend model, the Q3 dummy equals the firm dummy for
CIK 0000896878 (Intuit). The 61-column design has rank 60. Its one-dimensional
null space has zero loading on time: the time slope remains identified, while
the two nuisance effects cannot be separated. Statsmodels uses its standard
Moore-Penrose least-squares solution. I leave that parameterisation and its
finite-sample covariance convention explicit and do not interpret the aliased
control coefficients. The pooled and 10-Q trend designs have full column rank.
